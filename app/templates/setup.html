Your private, ad-free podcast proxy. Everything stays on this Mac.

  1. 1 Choose where the podcast library lives.
  2. 2 Set the address your phone can reach.
  3. 3 Install the transcription toolchain and Whisper model.
What gets installed

The app installs ffmpeg and whisper.cpp with Homebrew, then downloads the ggml-large-v3-turbo-q5_0 model (~547 MB) on first run. The model is not bundled with the app. You can change the model path later.
